How this actually works

This is signal processing, not a trained separation model — there's no AI here, which is exactly why it can run for free in your browser instead of on a paid server. Instrumental cancels out whatever is identical in the left and right channels, which is usually the lead vocal when it's mixed dead-center. Vocals keeps only that shared center signal, filtered down to the vocal frequency range. Bass and Other are a clean low/high split at 180 Hz. The separation strength slider pushes the center-channel cancellation harder above 1.0×, which removes more of the vocal (and anything else mixed dead-center) at the cost of a more hollow, phasey sound on what's left — worth nudging up on tracks where vocals still bleed through, and worth leaving at 1.0× when the instrumental sounds too thin. It won't cleanly lift drums out of a busy mix the way a paid neural separator will, but for karaoke tracks, quick acapellas, and isolating a bassline, it holds up well — and it costs nothing because your audio never leaves this tab.

Runs fully client-side with the Web Audio API. No account, no upload, no server.
