How this actually works

This runs real signal processing in your browser, not a trained separation model — no AI, which is why it's free with no server involved. For every moment and every frequency in the track, it compares the left and right channels: content that matches closely between them — usually the lead vocal, mixed dead-center — gets pulled into Vocals; everything left over becomes Instrumental. That's a frequency-by-frequency comparison (a short-time Fourier transform under the hood), not one blanket subtraction across the whole mix, so it holds up better than a basic karaoke filter and leaks less of the rest of the mix into the vocal. Bass and Other are then a clean low/high split of the instrumental at 180 Hz. The separation strength slider controls how strict the left/right match has to be — push it up on tracks where vocals still bleed through, at the cost of a slightly more processed-sounding result. It still won't cleanly lift drums out of a dense mix the way a paid neural separator will, but for isolating vocals, building karaoke tracks, and pulling out a bassline, it's well beyond a simple phase-cancellation trick — and your audio never leaves this tab.

Runs fully client-side with the Web Audio API. No account, no upload, no server.
