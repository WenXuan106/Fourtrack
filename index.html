How the iPad-safe AI works

Fourtrack loads the real HTDemucs ONNX model through ONNX Runtime Web. The model is run with single-threaded WASM on iPad, one roughly 7.8-second chunk at a time. Predictions are overlap-added, converted to 16-bit PCM, and written to temporary IndexedDB pieces. That avoids keeping four full-length Float32 stem arrays in RAM. The final WAV is assembled only after processing finishes.

Runs client-side with Web Audio + ONNX Runtime Web. No account and no audio upload to a Fourtrack server.
