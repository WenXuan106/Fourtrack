HTDemucs browser engine

The AI mode uses an HTDemucs ONNX model through ONNX Runtime Web. The model expects stereo 44.1 kHz audio in 343,980-sample chunks. Longer songs are processed with overlapping chunks and overlap-add reconstruction.

HTDemucs is much more demanding than the built-in DSP mode. The first AI run downloads roughly 166 MB of model data, and longer songs can require a large amount of RAM. Shorter tracks are recommended on iPad/mobile devices.
